About us

Calm rooms, considered down to the hinge.

We start with how you spend your day, then work backwards to layout, light, materials and furniture. The result is a space that looks composed and still works on a Tuesday morning.

Every project is led by an interior designer with 3+ years of experience managing premium residential interiors, from first sketch to final styling.

Process

How a project runs

  1. Listen

    We visit the space, learn how you use it and agree a brief and budget.

    1–2 weeks

  2. Sketch

    Floor plans, mood boards and material samples. You review and we refine.

    3–5 weeks

  3. Build

    We coordinate contractors and makers and visit the site every week.

    Depends on scope

  4. Style & hand over

    Furniture arrives, lighting is set and we walk you through the finished room.

    Final 2 weeks
